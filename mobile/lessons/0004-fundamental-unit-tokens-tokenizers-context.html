Lesson 0004 • Core Foundations

The Fundamental Unit of AI

Why LLMs do not read strings, how Byte-Pair Encoding works, and computing context costs on GCP.

In software engineering and Linux systems, text is represented as raw bytes or UTF-8 strings. When you run cat file.txt | wc -c, you are counting ASCII or Unicode bytes. But when working with LLMs on Google Cloud, you will immediately notice that all pricing, latency, and context limits are measured in Tokens.

What is a token, why does AI use them, and why does this matter for a DevOps engineer sizing cloud pipelines?

Why Can't Neural Networks Read Strings Directly?

Computers do not do math on letters. A CPU or GPU executing matrix multiplications requires numbers. There are two naive approaches to converting text to numbers, both of which fail:

  • Naive Approach 1: Character-by-Character (ASCII IDs). Treat every character as an integer (e.g., 'A'=65).
    Why it fails: A 50-page SAR report would be 150,000 individual characters long. The neural network would spend massive compute simply trying to figure out that 's-u-s-p-i-c-i-o-u-s' forms a single word.
  • Naive Approach 2: Whole Words. Give every unique word in the English language a unique integer ID.
    Why it fails: The vocabulary dictionary would be infinite. What happens when a suspect misspells a name, or when an account number like ACC_98012489 appears? The model would throw an "Out of Vocabulary" error.

The Engineering Solution: Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE)

Modern LLMs use Sub-word Tokenization (most commonly Byte-Pair Encoding). Think of BPE like a dynamic compression algorithm (similar to gzip or Huffman coding):

  1. Frequently used words become a single token (e.g., "transferred" → Token #19421).
  2. Rare words, SWIFT codes, or account numbers are broken into sub-word chunks (e.g., "Smurfing" → "Sm" + "urfing").
  3. Unseen characters gracefully fall back to raw byte tokens.

DevOps Pitfall: Numbers and Code Explode Token Counts
Notice how $9,850 and CHASUS33 fractured into multiple tokens. In banking data, tabular account numbers, timestamps, and hashes consume 2x to 3x more tokens than plain English sentences!

Rule of Thumb for Capacity Planning

When budgeting GCP Vertex AI costs and estimating prompt limits:

  • 1 token ≈ 4 characters of English text (or roughly 0.75 words).
  • 1,000 tokens ≈ 750 words (about 2 single-spaced pages of an AML report).
  • 1 million tokens ≈ 750,000 words (roughly 1,500 pages of text).

Measuring Tokens via the Vertex AI API

Before sending a 50MB payload to Gemini 1.5, DevOps pipelines should call the lightweight, free countTokens API to verify it fits within SLA limits:

curl -X POST \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $(gcloud auth print-access-token)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  "https://us-central1-aiplatform.googleapis.com/v1/projects/${PROJECT_ID}/locations/us-central1/publishers/google/models/gemini-1.5-flash:countTokens" \
  -d '{
    "contents": [{
      "role": "user",
      "parts": [{ "text": "Investigate wire transfer TX-90214 to shell corp." }]
    }]
  }'

• Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units — Sennrich et al. (ACL 2016).
