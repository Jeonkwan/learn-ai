Lesson 0006 • Ingestion & Chunking

Chunking Architectures & Boundary Strategies

Why arbitrary character splits fracture evidence, and how hierarchical chunking preserves context.

Now that Document AI has parsed our documents into clean text and structured markdown tables, we face a crucial engineering decision: How do we slice this text into pieces?

This process is called Chunking. In financial crime intelligence, chunking is not just a preprocessing utility; it directly determines whether your vector search discovers a money laundering scheme or misses it completely.

The Chunk Sizing Goldilocks Dilemma

  • If chunks are too small (e.g., 50 tokens): The embedding is hyper-specific, but lacks sufficient context. If Chunk A contains "Wire transfer of $5M" and Chunk B contains "sent to sanctioned entity BelGaz", neither chunk alone contains the full crime.
  • If chunks are too large (e.g., 4,000 tokens): The embedding model averages out dozens of different topics across 8 pages into a blurry, generic vector. Specific needle-in-a-haystack transactions get drowned out.
  • The Sweet Spot: Typically 300 to 500 tokens for narrative text, with a 10% to 15% sliding overlap.

The Sliding Window Overlap Mechanics

What happens if a crucial sentence falls right across the dividing line between Chunk 1 and Chunk 2? Without an overlap, the beginning of the sentence is in Chunk 1 and the end is in Chunk 2—fracturing the meaning in both vector embeddings.

Chunk 1 (Tokens 1 to 500):
"...Subject incorporated three holding entities in Nicosia. Over a 30-day period, funds were structured through accounts at Branch #12. [SLIDING OVERLAP BEGINS: The ultimate beneficiary of all three entities was identified as foreign national Boris K.]"
Chunk 2 (Tokens 450 to 950):
"[SLIDING OVERLAP CONTINUES: The ultimate beneficiary of all three entities was identified as foreign national Boris K.] Further examination revealed that Boris K. utilized nominee directors to circumvent Western energy sanctions..."

The 3 Production Chunking Strategies

Strategy How It Operates Best Used For
1. Recursive Character Splitting Tries splitting by paragraphs (\n\n). If a paragraph is still larger than 500 tokens, it splits by sentences (\n, . ), and only splits words as an absolute last resort. Industry standard default for investigative write-ups and SAR narratives.
2. Document-Aware (Markdown) Splitting Splits text based on structural markdown headers (# Section, ## Subsection) and keeps entire table blocks together in a single chunk. Compliance manuals, regulatory statutes, and Document AI output.
3. Hierarchical (Parent-Child) Chunking Slices text into tiny child chunks (100 tokens) for search indexing, but links them to their 1,000-token parent passage. Vector search matches the child, but injects the full parent into the LLM context. Complex legal depositions where fine-grained precision must be paired with broader context.
DevOps Takeaway: Preserving Metadata at the Chunk Level
Every chunk stored in your pipeline must carry rich metadata: case_id, source_file_gcs_uri, page_number, and chunk_index. This enables downstream filtering and audit logging.
