Lesson 0012 • Generation & Grounding

Prompt Augmentation & Context Assembly

Mitigating the 'Lost in the Middle' effect, deterministic generation, and calling Gemini on Vertex AI.

We have arrived at the final stage of the Vector Pipeline: Augmentation & Generation. Our retrieval engine has filtered out millions of noise chunks and handed us the 3 most relevant evidence passages.

Now, how do we assemble these passages into a prompt for Gemini 1.5 on Google Cloud such that the output is 100% auditable and free of hallucinations?

The "Lost in the Middle" Attention Curve

In 2023, Stanford and UC Berkeley researchers discovered a critical flaw in transformer attention mechanisms: LLMs remember facts placed at the very beginning and the very end of the context window, but frequently overlook facts placed in the middle.

The U-Shaped Attention Curve
If you provide 10 chunks of context, the model will pay strong attention to Chunks #1, #2, #9, and #10, while practically ignoring Chunks #4 through #7!

DevOps Mitigation: Always place your #1 highest-scoring re-ranked chunk at the very top, place your #2 chunk at the very bottom, and place instructions at the bookends.

The Production Financial Crime Prompt Template

In high-stakes banking environments, the system prompt must strictly constrain the model to cite its sources and refuse to speculate:

SYSTEM PROMPT: You are a Senior Financial Crime Intelligence Assistant for an AML unit. Strict Operational Directives: 1. Answer the query using ONLY the verified evidence excerpts below. 2. Every factual claim MUST include a bracketed citation to the Source File and Chunk ID (e.g., [SAR_2024_09.pdf#chunk_042]). 3. If the provided excerpts do not contain the answer, state: "I cannot find evidence in the archive." DO NOT speculate or invent facts. VERIFIED EVIDENCE EXCERPTS: --- [Source: SAR_2024_09.pdf | Chunk: 042 | Relevance: 0.96] "Subject deposited structured cash amounts ($9,800, $9,500) beneath the $10,000 threshold..." --- [Source: Wire_Ledger_2024.pdf | Chunk: 118 | Relevance: 0.91] "Account 9901 transferred $19,300 to Mediterranean Luxury Yachts Ltd on Oct 4." --- INVESTIGATOR QUERY: Did Account 9901 engage in structuring and where did the proceeds go?

Invoking Vertex AI Gemini 1.5 via curl

Notice the two crucial parameters in the API payload:

  • temperature: 0.0 → Eliminates random token sampling. The model greedily chooses the most mathematically probable tokens, maximizing factual precision.
  • topP: 0.1 → Restricts token selection to high-probability tokens.
curl -X POST \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $(gcloud auth print-access-token)" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  "https://us-central1-aiplatform.googleapis.com/v1/projects/${PROJECT_ID}/locations/us-central1/publishers/google/models/gemini-1.5-flash:generateContent" \
  -d '{
    "contents": [{
      "role": "user",
      "parts": [{ "text": "...[INSERT ASSEMBLED PROMPT HERE]..." }]
    }],
    "generationConfig": {
      "temperature": 0.0,
      "maxOutputTokens": 1024,
      "topP": 0.1
    }
  }'
